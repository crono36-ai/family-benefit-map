An indicator, not an eligibility count

The workbook reports FTB Part A payment recipients, not children. FTB is paid to the family, and families can have different numbers of children. This snapshot cannot establish how many children are eligible for the Child Dental Benefits Schedule (CDBS), their dental needs, or use of benefits.

CDBS also requires Medicare eligibility, age 0–17 for at least one day of the calendar year, and an eligible payment in that year. Other qualifying payments apply. A point-in-time count does not establish annual eligibility.
